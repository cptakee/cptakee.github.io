PicFit — Delete Account / Data

AKEgames inc · Last updated: October 2026

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open PicFit
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap Delete My Account (or Delete Account)
  4. Confirm — this permanently erases local progress and signed-in account data associated with the app on your device

By email

If you cannot use the in-app option, email us from the address linked to your account:

akegamesinc@gmail.com

Subject: PicFit Account Deletion Request

Include: your Google Play / App Store email (if known) and that you want your PicFit account and related data deleted. We process requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Cloud account / profile data tied to PicFit sign-in (when applicable)
  • Local game progress and settings after in-app delete or uninstall + clear data

Purchases handled by Google Play / Apple remain subject to their store policies. Advertising partners may retain device identifiers under their own policies until you reset advertising ID in system settings.
